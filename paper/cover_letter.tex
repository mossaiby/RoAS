\vspace{0.5em}
Dear Editor-in-Chief,

I am pleased to submit the above-titled original research paper for consideration for publication in \emph{Neurocomputing}.

The manuscript develops a common output-space read abstraction for finite attention, external retrieval, and parameter-expert routing. Its main contributions are:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=0.3em]
  \item a measure-theoretic definition with explicit existence conditions and support for query-dependent expert outputs;
  \item exact ranked top-$k$ bounds, an error-budgeted adaptive rule, and an approximate signed-cancellation bound; and
  \item controlled synthetic and CounterFact studies of external memory, ANN candidates, routing, cancellation, and GPT-2 steering.
\end{enumerate}

The experiments are intentionally presented as mechanism studies. Trainable synthetic experiments use five seeds, while the manuscript explicitly identifies the absence of large-scale ANN evaluation, competitive end-to-end model-editing baselines, and certified unlearning as limitations. Code, benchmark definitions, result files, and plotting scripts are available at \url{https://github.com/mossaiby/RoAS}.

I confirm that:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=0.2em]
  \item the manuscript is original and is not under consideration elsewhere;
  \item the author declares no competing financial or personal interests; and
  \item the use of generative AI during manuscript preparation is disclosed in the manuscript according to Elsevier policy.
\end{itemize}

The paper may be relevant to readers interested in neural memory, retrieval, sparse routing, and mathematical descriptions of attention. Thank you for considering the manuscript.
