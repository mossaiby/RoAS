\section*{Research Highlights}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Weighted neural reads are expressed over valued measurable atom spaces.
  \item Exact top-$k$ read truncation has two exponential error bounds.
  \item CounterFact-500 exposes strong retrieval but weak thresholded paraphrase recall.
  \item Exact tail mass selects $k$ under a certified vector-error budget.
  \item Signed-read perturbation bounds quantify approximate cancellation failure.
  \item Five-seed matched routing exposes corpus-to-parameter confusion.
\end{itemize}
