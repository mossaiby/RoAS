\section*{Research Highlights}

\begin{itemize}
  \item One read operator over atom spaces covers attention, retrieval, and MoE routing
  \item A null atom yields abstention; the retrieval threshold is its zero-temperature limit
  \item An anti-atom is deletion plus a null mass: it abstains where deletion falls through
  \item A query-side margin certifies a top-1 radius at insertion with no Lipschitz premise
  \item A rank-margin gate lifts CounterFact paraphrase activation to 71\% at 7\% false hits
\end{itemize}
