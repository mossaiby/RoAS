\title{Reads over Atom Spaces:\\
A Mathematical View of Context, Retrieval, and Parameter Routing in Language Models}

\author[inst1]{Farshid Mossaiby\corref{cor1}}
\ead{mossaiby@eng.ui.ac.ir}
\cortext[cor1]{Corresponding author.}
\affiliation[inst1]{organization={Department of Civil Engineering, University of Isfahan}, postcode={81744-73441}, city={Isfahan}, country={Iran}}

\begin{abstract}
Attention, external retrieval, and parameter-expert routing all compute query-conditioned weighted reads. We provide a common analytical lens based on valued atom spaces $(\Om,\mathcal F,\nu,v)$ and the read operator $R(x;\Om)=\int_\Om v(\omega)\,d\mu_x(\omega)$, which reduces to a softmax-weighted value combination under a counting measure. Operations on the reference measure $\nu$ express several mechanisms in one place. A \emph{null atom} yields abstention, and the hard activation threshold of gated retrieval is its zero-temperature limit. A matched \emph{anti-atom} is exactly deletion plus a null mass at the deleted key. A query-side margin certifies, at insertion time and without a Lipschitz premise, a radius within which a record remains the top-1 read. An exact softmax-tail bound gives an error-budgeted truncation rule that selects 52--86\% of a synthetic corpus at $N=25{,}000$, so it is a certificate rather than a sublinear method. On CounterFact-500, held-out calibration of a frozen-MiniLM activation threshold raises paraphrase activation from 38.2\% to 53.2\% at a false-activation cost of 9.6\% to 16.1\%. The remaining gap to 91\% top-1 recall consists of correctly ranked, low-similarity paraphrases: a gate on the rank margin reaches 70.8\% activation at 7.0\% false activation. Under one scorer, the gated read, a rank-one parametric reference, and GRACE occupy different points of a generalization--locality trade-off set by their key spaces. Tri-space routing with null atoms and first-token steering are reported as diagnostics. We offer a shared vocabulary and empirical calibrations of what retrieval, margin, and tail arguments can certify, not a new editing method.
\end{abstract}
